🏫 About Chamboli Secondary School

Founded in January 1965 in Kitwe, Copperbelt Province, Chamboli Secondary School carries a rich history of academic excellence and leadership development. The school offers complete secondary education following the Examinations Council of Zambia (ECZ) national curriculum. We foster intellectual growth, creativity, and personal discipline across all junior and senior grade levels.

📚 Academic Curriculum & Subjects Offered

We provide a comprehensive range of subjects tailored to both national examinations and practical career pathways:

Junior Secondary (Forms 1 & 2)

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Sciences
  • Social Studies
  • Religious Education
  • Computer Studies / ICT
  • Business Studies

Senior Secondary (Grades 10 – 12)

  • English Language & Literature
  • Mathematics & Additional Maths
  • Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • Civic Education & History
  • Geography & Religious Education
  • Commerce & Principles of Accounts
  • Agricultural Science
